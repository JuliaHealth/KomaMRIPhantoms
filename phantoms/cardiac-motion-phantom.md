This phantom represents a healthy adult four-chamber heart at 3 T, combining CT-derived anatomy with separate myocardium, blood, and surrounding fat compartments, tissue-specific MR properties, and a synthetic periodic cardiac cycle. Its anatomical labels, dense spin sampling, and near-incompressible myocardial motion make it useful for reproducible KomaMRI studies of cardiac contrast, slice planning, sequence design, and motion artifacts.
